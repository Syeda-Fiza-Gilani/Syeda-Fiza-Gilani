## 👋 About Me
I'm a full-stack developer who likes building complete products with Next.js, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS on the frontend, and authentication (NextAuth), PostgreSQL, and Prisma behind it. I also add AI features like RAG and agents using FastAPI, Qdrant, and Gemini.

## 🚀 Projects

### 🧠 AI Agents & RAG
- [**WhatsApp AI Sales Agent**](https://github.com/Syeda-Fiza-Gilani/WhatsApp-AI-Sales-Agent-with-RAG): replies to text, voice, and images using a product catalogue
- [**Aura-Control**](https://github.com/Syeda-Fiza-Gilani/Aura-Control): WhatsApp assistant that handles routine replies and flags sensitive ones for me
- [**Ingestion Pipeline**](https://github.com/Syeda-Fiza-Gilani/ingestion-pipeline): RAG setup with n8n, Qdrant, and Gemini
- [**AI Receptionist**](https://github.com/Syeda-Fiza-Gilani/AI-Receptionist): voice agent that answers calls and books on Google Calendar
- [**AI Email Assistant**](https://github.com/Syeda-Fiza-Gilani/ai-email-assistant-approval): drafts replies and waits for my approval before sending

### 🔄 Lead & Email Automation
- [**Zero-Leak Lead Router**](https://github.com/Syeda-Fiza-Gilani/zero-leak-lead-router-with-calendar-booking): qualifies leads, syncs HubSpot, alerts Slack, books meetings
- [**Enriched Lead Router**](https://github.com/Syeda-Fiza-Gilani/n8n-enriched-lead-router): web enrichment, Gemini scoring, HubSpot, Slack
- [**AI Lead Triage Pipeline**](https://github.com/Syeda-Fiza-Gilani/AI-Lead-Triage-Dynamic-State-Migration-Pipeline): n8n workflow with custom JS nodes and Gemini
- [**Email Lead Triage**](https://github.com/Syeda-Fiza-Gilani/Email-Lead-Triage): classifies emails, logs to CRM, pings Slack on hot leads
- [**Gmail Lead Capture**](https://github.com/Syeda-Fiza-Gilani/Gmail-Lead-Capture): sends Gmail leads to HubSpot with sales alerts
- [**B2B Web Crawler**](https://github.com/Syeda-Fiza-Gilani/B2B-Web-Crowler): pulls contacts from company sites into Sheets and HubSpot
- [**Email Follow-Up Campaigns**](https://github.com/Syeda-Fiza-Gilani/Email-Follow-Up-Campaigns): follow-ups that stop when someone replies
